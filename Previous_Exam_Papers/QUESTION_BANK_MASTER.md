## 2. All Transcribed Exam Questions by Subject Pillar

### Pillar 1: Earth Systems, Spheres & Planetary Boundaries
- **Grasshopper Effect / Global Distillation** (Mid-1 2024, Mid-1 2022 [3M]): Volatile persistent organic pollutants (POPs) evaporating in warm equatorial regions and condensing/depositing as toxic precipitation in cold polar regions.
- **Hypercane vs Present Hurricanes** (Mid-1 2024 [2M], End 2022 [1M]): Hypothetical megastorm formed over ocean waters >50°C, injecting water vapor and chlorofluorocarbons directly into the stratosphere, destroying ozone layer.
- **Volcanic Eruptions & Deglaciation** (Mid-1 2024 [2M], Mid-2 2018 [2M]): Melting glacial ice relieves lithospheric overburden pressure, enabling magma decompression and increased explosive volcanism.
- **Aral Sea Degradation** (Mid-1 2018 [3M], Quiz-1 2023 [4M]): Soviet diversion of Amu Darya and Syr Darya for cotton farming causing 90% water loss, salt storms within 300 km radius, and fishing industry collapse.
- **Eutrophication & Mississippi Dead Zone** (Quiz-1 2023 [3M], End 2022 [1M]): Agricultural N & P runoff causing excessive algal blooms, bacterial decomposition, severe oxygen depletion (BOD spike), and hypoxic dead zones.

### Pillar 2: Carbon Cycle, Sinks & Market Mechanisms
- **Biomass Carbon Formula** (Mid-2 2018 [0.5M], End 2019 [0.5M], End 2022 [1M]): Dry biomass of wood contains exactly ~50% (0.5x) carbon by weight ($1 \text{ kg dry wood} = 0.5 \text{ kg carbon}$).
- **Kyoto Protocol vs Paris Agreement** (End 2024 [2M], End 2023 [2M], End 2022 [2M]): Kyoto imposed legally binding reduction targets exclusively on Annex-I industrialized nations; Paris established universal nationally determined contributions (NDCs) across all nations.
- **Clean Development Mechanism (CDM)** (Mid-2 2018 [0.5M], Quiz-3 2020 [0.5M], End 2022 [1M]): Nuclear projects are strictly ineligible under CDM; generates Certified Emission Reductions (CERs).

### Pillar 3: Technology Solutions, Green Computing & Engineering
- **Aether Air-to-Diamond Process** (End 2023 [3M], End 2022 [5M]): Climeworks DAC CO2 -> catalytic conversion to CH4 -> CVD vacuum plasma reactor at extreme temperatures -> raw diamond lattice -> cut in Surat India (offsets 20t CO2/carat).
- **Novacem vs Portland Cement** (End 2023 [3M], End 2019 [3M], Mid-2 2018 [1.5M]): Portland emits ~0.9 kg CO2/kg cement via limestone calcination at 1450°C; Novacem uses magnesium silicates at 650°C and carbonates ambient CO2 ($MgO + CO_2 \rightarrow MgCO_3$) sequestering ~100 kg CO2 net per tonne.
- **Thin Client vs Thick Client** (End 2024 [2M], End 2023 [4M], End 2022 [2M], End 2019 [4M]): Thin client has no local disk, consumes ~5-15W, relies on central virtualization; Thick client has local OS/storage, consumes ~75-150W, high embodied carbon.
- **Green Engineering 12 Principles** (End 2024 [2M], End 2023 [4M], End 2019 [3M], End 2018 [6M]): Inherent rather than circumstantial; Prevention rather than treatment; Conserve complexity; Integrate local material/energy flows (Kalundborg industrial symbiosis).
- **Denim Manufacturing Environmental Footprint** (End 2024 [2M], End 2023 [4M], End 2022 [2M], End 2019 [4M]): 7,500L water per pair, toxic synthetic indigo effluent in Pearl River, worker silicosis from manual sandblasting.

### Pillar 4: Environmental Governance, Law, Accounting & Ethics
- **SNA vs SEEA Asset Classification** (End 2024 [2M], End 2023 [2M], End 2022 [10M], End 2019 [10M], End 2018 [10M]): Standard National Accounts (SNA) only counts marketed production ($GDP = C+I+G+X-M$) and treats forest clearcutting as pure positive income; SEEA creates natural capital balance sheets, tracks physical flows (residuals/ecosystem inputs), and accounts for environmental depreciation.
- **EIA Process & Great Nicobar Case** (End 2023 [2M], End 2022 [10M], End 2018 [10M]): Phase 1 Screening (triage into low, moderate, high risk) vs Phase 2 Full EIA study; Expert Appraisal Committee (EAC) 11-member composition; baseline data omission penalties.
- **Anthropocentrism vs Biocentrism vs Ecocentrism** (End 2024 [2M], End 2023 [3M], End 2022 [5M], End 2018 [6M]): Anthropocentrism (human utility only); Biocentrism (all living organisms have intrinsic moral rights); Ecocentrism (holistic biotic community, Leopold Land Ethic).
- **Indian Environmental Movements** (End 2023 [1M], Mid-1 2018 [5M], End 2019 [2M]): 1730 Bishnoi Khejarli massacre (Amrita Devi, 363 martyrs); 1973 Chipko (Gaura Devi, Sunderlal Bahuguna 'Ecology is permanent economy'); 1983 Appiko (Pandurang Hegde); Silent Valley (KSSP, Lion-tailed macaque).
