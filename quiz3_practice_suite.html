Lecture 11 • Enhanced Weathering High Yield

Enhanced Silicate Rock Weathering (Olivine)

$$Mg_2SiO_4 + 4CO_2 + 4H_2O \to 2Mg^{2+} + 4HCO_3^- + H_4SiO_4$$

Micro-pulverized olivine applied to agricultural topsoil reacts with soil carbonic acid, converting gaseous $CO_2$ into soluble bicarbonate ($HCO_3^-$) washed to oceans, locking carbon for $>100,000 ext{ years}$.

Lecture 10 • Marine Chemistry Must Memorize

Ocean Acidification & Carbonate Depletion

$$CO_2(g) + H_2O \rightleftharpoons H_2CO_3 \rightleftharpoons H^+ + HCO_3^- \rightleftharpoons 2H^+ + CO_3^{2-}$$ $$H^+ + CO_3^{2-} \rightleftharpoons HCO_3^-$$

Excess $H^+$ consumes free carbonate ions ($CO_3^{2-}$), dropping the mineral saturation state ($\Omega < 1$) and causing marine shells ($CaCO_3$) to dissolve.

Lecture 11 • Aether Diamonds & DAC Exotic Technology

Sabatier Methanation (Air-to-Diamonds Pipeline)

$$CO_2 + 4H_2 \to CH_4 + 2H_2O \quad (\Delta H = -165\text{ kJ/mol})$$

Captured ambient $CO_2$ reacts with green hydrogen ($H_2$) to synthesize pure methane ($CH_4$), which is cracked in microwave plasma CVD reactors to grow gem diamonds.

Lecture 10 • Cleantech Materials Carbon Negative

Novacem Carbon-Negative Cement Carbonation

$$MgO + CO_2 + 2H_2O \to MgCO_3 \cdot 3H_2O$$

Produced from magnesium silicates at $650^\circ ext{C}$ (vs Portland limestone at $1450^\circ ext{C}$). Hardens by absorbing atmospheric $CO_2$, sequestering $0.6 ext{ t } CO_2$ per ton cement.

Lecture 11 • Thermodynamics Core Theory

Thermodynamic Minimum Work of Gas Separation (Sherwood Plot)

$$W_{\min} = -RT \left[ \ln x_{CO_2} + \frac{1 - x_{CO_2}}{x_{CO_2}} \ln(1 - x_{CO_2}) \right]$$

Because $CO_2$ is highly dilute in air ($x_{CO_2} \approx 0.042\% = 420\text{ ppm}$) compared to power plant flue gas ($12\text{–}15\%$), the theoretical minimum separation work is $\sim 3\times$ higher ($\sim 20\text{ kJ/mol}$ vs $\sim 7\text{ kJ/mol}$), requiring colossal contactor fan arrays.
