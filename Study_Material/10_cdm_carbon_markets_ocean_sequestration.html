Clean Development Mechanism (CDM) & Ocean Sequestration

Kyoto Flexible Instruments, Additionality Rubrics, and Oceanic Carbon Injection
💡 Why This Topic Matters:
The Clean Development Mechanism (Article 12 of the Kyoto Protocol) pioneered international market-based carbon finance, enabling Annex I developed nations to invest in emission-reduction projects in developing nations in exchange for Certified Emission Reductions (CERs). Concurrently, the ocean—Earth's largest active carbon pool—has been investigated for ocean iron fertilization and direct liquid $CO_2$ injection, raising complex ecological questions regarding ocean acidification and marine food web collapse.
Carbon Finance CDM & Certified Emission Reductions (CERs)

1 CER = 1 metric ton of $CO_2$ equivalent reduced. Projects must prove strict 'Additionality': emissions with the project are lower than what would have occurred in the baseline scenario without carbon revenue.

Ocean Pumps Ocean Carbon Pumps

1. Solubility pump (cooling surface water absorbs $CO_2$ and sinks at poles). 2. Biological pump (phytoplankton photosynthesis and sinking particulate organic carbon POC / marine snow).

Ocean Engineering Deep Ocean Injection & Acidification

Injecting liquid $CO_2$ at depths $>3000\,\text{m}$ (where liquid $CO_2$ is denser than seawater and forms clathrate hydrate lakes). Risks: significant localized pH drops, dissolution of calcareous plankton, and benthic toxicity.

Core Mechanics

Carbon Chemistry & Ocean Acidification Reactions

When gaseous or liquid $CO_2$ dissolves in seawater, it establishes a series of chemical equilibria:

$$CO_2\text{(aq)} + H_2O \rightleftharpoons H_2CO_3 \rightleftharpoons H^+ + HCO_3^- \rightleftharpoons 2H^+ + CO_3^{2-}$$

The release of free hydrogen ions ($H^+$) consumes carbonate ions ($CO_3^{2-}$):

$$H^+ + CO_3^{2-} \rightleftharpoons HCO_3^-$$

This drives down the carbonate saturation state ($\Omega$):

$$\Omega = \frac{[Ca^{2+}][CO_3^{2-}]}{K'_{sp}}$$

When $\Omega < 1.0$, water becomes undersaturated and corrosive to calcium carbonate shells of corals, pteropods, and coccolithophores.

Clean Development Mechanism (CDM) Architectural Flowchart

👁️ What the Diagram Shows: A complete operational flowchart showing financial and credit flows between Annex I developed investor countries and non-Annex I developing host nations under the Kyoto Protocol.
🔬 Scientific & Environmental Mechanism: Developed nations provide capital investment and clean technology to build emission-reduction projects in developing nations. The quantified emission reductions are verified by independent Designated Operational Entities (DOEs) and issued by the UN as Certified Emission Reductions (CERs).
📊 Quantitative / Mathematical Model: CER Generation Formula: $CER = BE - PE - LE$, where $BE$ is baseline emissions under business-as-usual, $PE$ is actual measured project emissions, and $LE$ is leakage occurring outside the project boundary.
⚠️ Exam Trap: To qualify, a project must demonstrate 'Financial Additionality'—meaning it would have been financially unviable without the anticipated revenue from carbon credit sales.
Figure 2

Ocean Carbon Pumps & Deep-Sea CO₂ Injection Thermodynamics

📍 Class-10b-Ocean Sequestration.pdf, Slide 8
🔍 Click diagram to zoom in full lightbox
👁️ What the Diagram Shows: A depth profile of the ocean showing atmospheric $CO_2$ dissolution, the biological pump (marine snow), and phase behavior of injected liquid $CO_2$.
🔬 Scientific & Environmental Mechanism: Liquid $CO_2$ is highly compressible compared to seawater. At depths less than ~3000 m, liquid $CO_2$ is less dense than surrounding water and floats upwards. Below 3000 m (pressures >300 atmospheres), liquid $CO_2$ becomes denser than seawater ($>1028\,\text{kg/m}^3$) and sinks to the seafloor, forming negative-buoyancy hydrate lakes.
📊 Quantitative / Mathematical Model: Density crossover depth: $\rho_{CO_2}(T, P) = \rho_{\text{seawater}}(T, P, S)$. Injected $CO_2$ dissolves into bottom water, forming carbonic acid: $CO_2 + H_2O \rightleftharpoons H^+ + HCO_3^-$, causing severe localized benthic acidification (pH drop of 0.5 to 1.5 units).
⚠️ Exam Trap: Deep ocean injection does not isolate carbon permanently forever; ocean thermohaline overturning will return deep water to the surface in approximately 500 to 1,000 years.
Rigorous Quantitative Model

CDM Baseline & Carbon Credit Formulas

$$CERs = \sum_t \left( BE_t - PE_t - LE_t \right), \quad \text{Additionality: } PE_t < BE_t$$

Where $BE_t$ is baseline emissions, $PE_t$ is project emissions, and $LE_t$ is leakage emissions occurring outside the project boundary.

Question (Click / Space to Flip)
What is the Additionality Principle in CDM projects?
🔄 Click card to reveal answer
Answer
The requirement that a project's greenhouse gas reductions must be additional to any that would occur in the absence of the certified CDM project activity (i.e., not business-as-usual).
Question (Click / Space to Flip)
What are High-Nutrient, Low-Chlorophyll (HNLC) ocean regions?
🔄 Click card to reveal answer
Answer
Ocean areas (such as the Southern Ocean and subarctic Pacific) with abundant nitrate and phosphate but low phytoplankton productivity due to the lack of the limiting trace micronutrient Iron ($Fe$).
Question (Click / Space to Flip)
Why does liquid $CO_2$ sink only below ~3,000 meters in the ocean?
🔄 Click card to reveal answer
Answer
Because liquid $CO_2$ is more compressible than seawater. Above ~3,000 m it is buoyant and rises; below ~3,000 m (pressure $> 30\,\text{MPa}$), its density exceeds that of seawater, allowing it to sink.
High-Yield Insights

Exam Traps, Fallacies & Professor Emphasis Points

Common student misconceptions and subtle distinctions tested in Midsem and Endsem exams.

  • ⚠️ Trap: Confusing CDM with Joint Implementation (JI). CDM involves developed Annex I nations investing in non-Annex I developing nations. Joint Implementation involves developed nations investing in other Annex I nations (typically economies in transition).
  • ⚠️ Exam Tip: Be ready to write out the carbonate equilibrium equations showing that adding $CO_2$ decreases free carbonate ion $[CO_3^{2-}]$ concentration, harming calcifying marine organisms.

Question 1
What is the numerical equivalence of one Certified Emission Reduction (CER) unit under the Kyoto Protocol?
A 1 metric ton of $CO_2$ equivalent reduced or sequestered
B 1,000 metric tons of pure methane
C 1 kilogram of sulfur hexafluoride
D 1 megawatt-hour of solar electricity
Explanation: By definition, 1 CER corresponds to exactly one metric ton of $CO_2$ equivalent ($1\,\text{t } CO_2e$) avoided or removed.
Question 2
What is the primary ecological risk associated with large-scale ocean iron fertilization?
A Immediate freezing of the ocean surface
B Deep-water oxygen depletion (hypoxia) during organic matter decay and potential release of powerful $N_2O$ gas
C Massive loss of oceanic salinity
D Accelerated solar ultraviolet penetration
Explanation: As massive stimulated blooms sink, heterotrophic bacteria consume dissolved oxygen to decompose the biomass, producing anoxic dead zones and generating nitrous oxide ($N_2O$, GWP=273).
