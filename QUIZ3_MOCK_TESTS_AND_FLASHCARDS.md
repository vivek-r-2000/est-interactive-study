### Q3. Differences (2 Marks each)
1. **Afforestation vs. Reforestation:**
   - *Afforestation:* Planting trees on land that historically has **not** contained forest cover for at least 50 years (e.g., degraded grassland, barren land).
   - *Reforestation:* Re-planting or regenerating trees on land that **previously supported forests** but was cleared due to deforestation, logging, or fire.
2. **Annex I vs. Annex II Countries:**
   - *Annex I:* Industrialized OECD countries plus Economies in Transition (EITs like Russia and Eastern Europe) that committed to binding emission reduction caps.
   - *Annex II:* A subset of Annex I (OECD nations only, excluding EITs) legally mandated to provide financial assistance and green technology transfer to developing (Non-Annex I) countries.

### Q4. Short Explanations (2.5 Marks each)
1. **Ocean Acidification:**
   - Chemical equilibrium:
     $$CO_2 + H_2O \rightleftharpoons H_2CO_3 \rightleftharpoons H^+ + HCO_3^-$$
     Excess $H^+$ reacts with carbonate ions: $H^+ + CO_3^{2-} \rightleftharpoons HCO_3^-$.
   - Ecological impact: Consuming $CO_3^{2-}$ reduces the mineral saturation state ($\Omega$) of calcite and aragonite below 1 ($\Omega < 1$). Organisms cannot precipitate calcium carbonate ($CaCO_3$) shells, causing existing shells to dissolve. Because pH is logarithmic ($-\log[H^+]$), a $0.1$ unit drop represents a $\sim 30\%$ increase in acidity.
2. **Urea Fertilization & Hazards:**
   - Mechanism: Adding synthetic urea ($\text{CO(NH}_2)_2$) provides bioavailable nitrogen to nitrogen-limited oceanic surface waters, prompting rapid phytoplankton blooms.
   - Hazards:
     1. *Deep Water Hypoxia:* Sinking dead organic matter is consumed by aerobic bacteria, stripping dissolved oxygen and creating dead zones.
     2. *Toxigenic Bloom Induction:* Promotes poisonous diatoms (*Pseudo-nitzschia*) that secrete lethal neurotoxins (**domoic acid**), poisoning marine mammals and entering commercial seafood.

### Q5. Agroforestry (3 Marks)
- **Definition (1M):** A sustainable land-use system integrating woody perennials (trees/shrubs) with agricultural crops and/or livestock on the same spatial plot.
- **Three Advantages (2M):**
  1. *Subsoil Sequestration:* Deep tree roots deposit carbon into deep subsoil horizons resistant to oxidation and tilling disturbance.
  2. *Food Security Alignment:* Farmers generate biogenic carbon sinks without taking fertile arable land out of food and grain production.
  3. *Fertilizer Reduction:* Nitrogen-fixing leguminous agro-trees enrich soil organic matter naturally, curbing synthetic fertilizer runoff and lowering agricultural $N_2O$ emissions.

### Q6. International Project Case Study (3 Marks)

  - Rehabilitated 25,000 hectares of logged, degraded Dipterocarp rainforest through enrichment planting with native dipterocarp wildings and climber cutting.
  - Sequestered millions of tonnes of $CO_2$ over a 60-year concession while restoring habitat for endangered orangutans and hornbills.
*(OR)*
- **Plan Vivo / Scolel'te Project (Chiapas, Mexico):**

  - Farmers develop individual farm management plans ('Plan Vivo') combining timber planting (cedar, mahogany), coffee shade agroforestry, and living fences.
  - Verified carbon credits sold on voluntary markets, directly funneling funding to poverty alleviation.

## 📋 MOCK EXAM 2: MODEL ANSWERS & MARKING SCHEME

### Section I
1. **Glacier Melt & Volcanism (2M):** Continental ice sheets weigh billions of tons, compressing the lithosphere. Deglaciation unloads this mass (isostatic rebound), causing mantle depressurization. Depressurization lowers magma melting temperatures and allows dissolved volatiles to nucleate into gas bubbles, drastically accelerating explosive volcanism.
2. **Hypercane Ozone Destruction (2M):** Reaching altitudes of $40\text{–}50\text{ km}$ directly into the stratosphere, hypercane columns inject massive plumes of water vapor and sea-salt aerosol chlorine radicals. In the stratosphere, reactive chlorine and hydroxyl radicals enter catalytic cycles that rapidly destroy ozone molecules ($O_3$).
3. **Global Terrestrial Stilling (2M):** Documented decadal slowdown in surface wind speeds (~0.3 m/s drop per decade). Caused by: (1) Increased surface roughness from forest regrowth and expanding urban structures; (2) Arctic amplification reducing the thermal equator-to-pole temperature gradient, weakening planetary pressure differentials.
4. **Sherwood Plot & DAC Thermodynamics (2M):** Thermodynamic minimum work of separation is inversely logarithmic to feed concentration:
   $$W_{\min} = -RT \left[ \ln x + \frac{1-x}{x}\ln(1-x) \right]$$
   Because atmospheric $CO_2$ is extremely dilute ($\sim 420\text{ ppm} = 0.042\%$) vs. power plant flue gas ($12\text{–}15\%$), separating 1 ton of $CO_2$ from air requires $\sim 3\times$ more theoretical energy and circulating $>1,000,000\text{ m}^3$ of air.
5. **Seaweed Enteric Mitigation (2M):** Active compound is **bromoform** ($CHBr_3$). It binds to and blocks the active site of the methyl-coenzyme M reductase enzyme in methanogenic archaea within the cow's rumen, halting methane synthesis by up to $80\text{–}90\%$.

### Section II
1. **Kyoto Flexible Mechanisms (3M):**
   - *CDM:* Annex I invests in Non-Annex I (developing); yields Certified Emission Reductions (CERs).
   - *JI:* Annex I invests in another Annex I (EITs); yields Emission Reduction Units (ERUs).
   - *IET:* Annex I nations trade assigned surplus allowance units (AAUs) directly between themselves.
2. **Portland vs. Novacem (3M):**
   - *Portland:* Raw material is limestone ($CaCO_3$); calcined at $1,450^\circ\text{C}$; releases calcination and fuel emissions emitting $0.8\text{–}1.0\text{ t } CO_2/\text{t cement}$.
   - *Novacem:* Raw material is magnesium silicate (talc/serpentine); calcined at low temperature ($650^\circ\text{C}$); absorbs $CO_2$ during curing ($MgO + CO_2 \to MgCO_3$); net negative sequestering $0.6\text{ t } CO_2/\text{t cement}$.
3. **Iron Hypothesis vs. Enhanced Weathering (4M):**
   - *Iron Hypothesis:* Fe is the limiting micronutrient in HNLC oceans; seeding Fe triggers diatom blooms that sink carbon into the abyss.
   - *Enhanced Weathering:* Spreading pulverized olivine/basalt ($<50\,\mu m$) across agricultural topsoil. Reacts with atmospheric $CO_2$ in soil rainwater:
     $$Mg_2SiO_4 + 4CO_2 + 4H_2O \to 2Mg^{2+} + 4HCO_3^- + H_4SiO_4$$
     Converts gaseous carbon into stable aqueous bicarbonate ($HCO_3^-$) which drains to oceans, locking carbon for $>100,000\text{ years}$.

### Section III
1. **Aether Diamonds & DAC Companies (5M):**
   - *Aether Pipeline (3M):* (1) Direct Air Capture extracts atmospheric $CO_2$; (2) Sabatier methanation reaction ($CO_2 + 4H_2 \to CH_4 + 2H_2O$) combines $CO_2$ with green hydrogen to synthesize high-purity methane; (3) Methane is fed into microwave Chemical Vapor Deposition (CVD) plasma reactors to grow gem-grade diamond crystals.
   - *DAC Companies (2M):*
     - **Climeworks:** Solid amine-functionalized cellulose filter matrix desorbing $CO_2$ at $\sim 100^\circ\text{C}$.
     - **Carbon Engineering:** Liquid alkaline solvent ($KOH \to K_2CO_3 \to CaCO_3$ pellets) requiring high-temperature natural gas/electric calcination at $900^\circ\text{C}$.
2. **Tipping Cascades & Black Swans (5M):**
   - *Black Swan (1M):* Unpredictable outlier event with massive systemic impacts, rationalized only in hindsight.
   - *Three Planetary Elements (4M):*
     1. *Arctic Summer Sea Ice Loss:* Melts albedo shield ($0.8 \to 0.06$), accelerating regional warming and permafrost collapse.
     2. *WAIS Collapse:* Marine Ice Sheet Instability (MISI) on reverse-sloping bedrock commits world to 3–5 meters of sea-level rise.
     3. *Amazon Savannization:* Deforestation $>20\text{–}25\%$ disrupts the atmospheric biotic moisture pump, collapsing rainfall recycling and converting rainforest to dry degraded scrubland, venting ~100 Gt C.
3. **Blue Carbon & Biochar Pyrolysis (5M):**
   - *Blue Carbon (2.5M):* Mangroves and salt marshes build organic-rich soil vertically as sea levels rise. Submerged under saline water, soils lack oxygen, suppressing aerobic fungal/bacterial decomposition. Sediments sequester carbon continuously for millennia without saturation.
   - *Biochar Pyrolysis (2.5M):* Thermal heating of biomass at $400^\circ\text{–}700^\circ\text{C}$ without $O_2$. Yields recalcitrant polycyclic aromatic carbon with soil half-lives $>1000\text{ years}$. Global potential: up to **4.8 Gt $CO_2$/year**, improving soil cation exchange capacity (CEC).

# PART II: ACTIVE RECALL FLASHCARDS (45 CARDS)

### Set 1: High-Frequency Traps & Distinctions
* **Card 01:**
  - *Front:* Are Nuclear energy or power projects eligible for CER carbon credits under the Clean Development Mechanism (CDM)?
  - *Back:* **NO.** Nuclear projects were explicitly banned under the Marrakech Accords due to weapon proliferation risks, radioactive waste disposal hazards, and safety concerns.
* **Card 02:**
  - *Front:* What is the fundamental difference between Annex I and Annex II nations under UNFCCC?
  - *Back:* Annex I includes all industrialized OECD nations + Economies in Transition (EITs). Annex II contains **only** OECD nations (excluding EITs) and is legally bound to provide financial and technical resources to Non-Annex I developing countries.
* **Card 03:**
  - *Front:* Contrast the compliance approach of the Kyoto Protocol with the Paris Agreement.
  - *Back:* Kyoto = Top-down, legally binding emission reduction targets imposed solely on Annex I developed countries. Paris Agreement = Bottom-up, universal consensus where all countries submit voluntary Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs).
* **Card 04:**
  - *Front:* Why does a mature ecological climax forest stop accumulating net carbon in standing biomass?
  - *Back:* Because Gross Primary Productivity (GPP) equals total ecosystem respiration (\(GPP = R_{\text{autotrophic}} + R_{\text{heterotrophic}}\)). Photosynthetic carbon gain is fully consumed by tree maintenance respiration and microbial decomposition of litterfall.
* **Card 05:**
  - *Front:* Differentiate between Afforestation and Reforestation under UNFCCC definitions.
  - *Back:* Afforestation = Establishing forest on land that has not had forest for $\ge 50\text{ years}$. Reforestation = Re-establishing trees on land that historically was forested but cleared recently through deforestation or logging.
* **Card 06:**
  - *Front:* How does Novacem cement differ from Portland cement in raw materials and net carbon footprint?
  - *Back:* Portland uses limestone ($CaCO_3$) heated to $1450^\circ\text{C}$, releasing $0.8\text{–}1.0\text{ t } CO_2/\text{t}$. Novacem uses magnesium silicates heated to only $650^\circ\text{C}$, absorbing $CO_2$ during curing to form $MgCO_3$ ($\text{net } -0.6\text{ t } CO_2/\text{t cement}$).
* **Card 07:**
  - *Front:* What is the difference between CER and ERU credits?
  - *Back:* **CER** (Certified Emission Reduction) is generated by CDM projects in Non-Annex I developing countries. **ERU** (Emission Reduction Unit) is generated by Joint Implementation (JI) projects in Annex I countries. Both equal $1\text{ t } CO_2\text{e}$.

### Set 4: Planetary Risks & Abnormal Phenomena
* **Card 22:**
  - *Front:* What is Eric Drexler's "Gray Goo" scenario?
  - *Back:* An existential disaster where self-replicating molecular nanotechnology assemblers multiply exponentially without restraint, consuming all planetary carbon and biosphere resources into copies of themselves.
* **Card 23:**
  - *Front:* What three attributes define a Taleb "Black Swan" event?
  - *Back:* (1) Unpredictable outlier; (2) Extreme catastrophic/transformative systemic impact; (3) Retrospectively rationalized as predictable after the fact.
* **Card 24:**
  - *Front:* What is the primary cause of "Ocean Darkening"?
  - *Back:* Accelerated runoff from thawing permafrost, increased storm rainfall, and soil erosion flushing dissolved organic matter (DOM) and humic acids into coastal waters, blocking sunlight.
* **Card 25:**
  - *Front:* Why does global warming increase lightning strike frequencies?
  - *Back:* Warmer air holds ~7% more moisture per $1^\circ\text{C}$ warming (Clausius-Clapeyron). Elevated atmospheric moisture increases convective instability (CAPE), building taller thunderheads where ice-graupel collisions generate intense electrical charge.
* **Card 26:**
  - *Front:* Explain the Marine Ice Sheet Instability (MISI) affecting the West Antarctic Ice Sheet (WAIS).
  - *Back:* The WAIS is grounded on bedrock sloping downward toward the continental interior below sea level. Warm circumpolar deep water melts the ice grounding line, triggering an irreversible runaway geometric retreat.
